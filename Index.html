🧭 Navigation Rules

  1. Click on the question number in the palette to jump to that question.
  2. Click Save & Next to save your answer and move to the next question.
  3. Click Mark for Review & Next to flag a question for later review.
  4. Click Clear Response to deselect your chosen answer.
  5. Important: Selecting an option alone will not save your answer. You must press Save & Next or Mark for Review & Next.
  6. If you mark a question for review without answering, it counts as Marked for Review only.
  7. If you answer and then mark for review, it becomes Answered & Marked and will be evaluated.

🎯 Marking Scheme

  1. Marks awarded based on each question's defined positive marks.
  2. Negative marking applied for incorrect answers as per question.
  3. Unattempted questions are awarded zero marks.
  4. Questions marked for review but answered will be evaluated.

⚠️ Important

  1. Do not refresh / close the browser. Auto-save is active but a refresh ends the live timer.
  2. The exam will auto-submit when the timer reaches 00:00:00.
  3. Keyboard Shortcuts: 1-6 select option, S Save & Next, M Mark Review, P Previous, C Clear, B Bookmark.
